# Product doctrine

Noema remains between structureless notes and rigid spreadsheets.

1. Raw human-authored entries are primary.
2. Structure is optional and progressive.
3. Capture intent is a hint, not a category.
4. One entry may support several capabilities.
5. Generated views are secondary projections.
6. Missing and uncertain information are valid.
7. Approximate and relative dates remain honest.
8. Historical truth survives current-value changes.
9. AI inference never silently creates canonical facts.
10. Capture remains fast enough for a ten-second entry.

This increment intentionally adds no automatic classification or date inference. The model retains source and confidence seams so later interpretations can remain explicit and reviewable.
