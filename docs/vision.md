# Life Corpus vision

Life Corpus is a capture-first personal life operating system. A thought becomes safe to forget as soon as its original wording is preserved. Classification, prioritization, and interpretation are later activities, not toll gates.

The system treats ambiguity as valid data, keeps human records canonical, and gives AI only bounded, inspectable, human-controlled authority. It should eventually support reasoned tradeoffs across continuity, recovery, health, maintenance, meaningful experiences, and creative ambition without reducing life to austerity.
